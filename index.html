1. Iran war \u2014 tanker lane may reopenAFFECTS \u00b7 XOM \u00b7 GLD \u00b7 VOO \u00b7 QQQ

War about six months old. Oil was ~$72 before it. Last week Brent near $110. Tuesday settle $99.25, fifth down day, after a dip under $98.

Iran said Hormuz could reopen in about seven days if the U.S. eases military pressure and lifts a port blockade. Hormuz is the narrow exit from the Persian Gulf. A large share of seaborne oil has to pass through it.

Saudi Arabia is testing the East-West Pipeline to the Red Sea (Yanbu), a bypass after Houthi damage this month. A tanker was hit in Hormuz on Monday. Trump defended the war at the U.N. Markets sold oil on the reopen talk. The war is not over.

Learn: oil has a price and a route. Close the lane and diesel, shipping, and inflation jump. Talk of opening it and traders sell first. Exxon follows the barrel. Gold follows war risk. VOO/QQQ follow inflation fear.

2. Ukraine hit another Russian refineryAFFECTS \u00b7 XOM \u00b7 VOO \u00b7 GLD

Year five. Front lines barely move. Russia struck at least four Ukrainian regions overnight. Ukraine damaged another Russian refinery, after a large weekend strike near Moscow.

Refineries turn crude into diesel and jet fuel. Smash them and crude can fall while diesel stays scarce. U.S. diesel was recently marked near $6.53 even as crude fell five days. Trump wants an energy ceasefire talk with Zelenskyy in New York.

Learn: crude is the raw barrel. Diesel is the refined product trucks burn. “Oil is down” is not “inflation is done.”

3. Fed already hiked \u2014 banks soldAFFECTS \u00b7 JPM \u00b7 VOO \u00b7 QQQ \u00b7 MSFT

First Fed hike in three years last week. Another hike in October is still on the table. 10-year yield 4.96% Tuesday, unchanged, after crossing 5% last week. 2-year auction was soft.

JPM fell about 3.2% and helped drag the Dow. Financials were the worst sector. Banks live on the gap between short rates and long rates. That gap is under pressure. Fed speakers kept pointing at services inflation, not just energy.

Learn: the Fed sets the overnight rate. The 10-year is the market’s vote. A hike already done can still hit bank stocks days later.
