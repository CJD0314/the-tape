WORLD NEWS

The world first. Then which watch-list names, if any, can feel it.

1. Iran war — tanker lane talk. Iran said the main oil shipping lane could reopen in about a week if the U.S. eases pressure. Saudi Arabia is testing a pipeline that bypasses that lane. A tanker was hit in the lane this week. Crude has fallen five days, back under $100, still far above ~$72 from before the war.

AFFECTS · XOM · GLD · VOO · QQQ

Learn: much of the world’s oil leaves the Persian Gulf through one narrow waterway. Close it and diesel and inflation jump. Talk of opening it and oil falls — until the next strike.

2. Ukraine hit another Russian oil refinery. Russia struck Ukrainian cities overnight. Diesel is expensive because of this war and the Iran war. Trump is meeting Zelenskyy and talking about an energy ceasefire.

AFFECTS · XOM · VOO · GLD

Learn: crude and diesel are not the same product. Cheaper crude can sit next to painful diesel if refineries are offline. “Oil is down” does not mean inflation is done.

3. The Fed raised rates last week. Another hike is still possible. 10-year yield 4.96%. Banks sold. JPM about −3%.

AFFECTS · JPM · VOO · QQQ · MSFT

Learn: the Fed sets the short-term rate. The 10-year is what the bond market thinks. A hike already done can keep hitting bank stocks days later.

4. U.N. week in New York. Xi due in the U.S. later this week. Trump addressed the General Assembly and is set to see leaders from the U.K., Japan, Ukraine, Venezuela, and the Gulf.

AFFECTS · none of the six yet

Learn: diplomacy is a calendar event. Markets reprice after a sentence changes oil or yields, not after the motorcade arrives.

5. U.S., Denmark, and Greenland signed an Arctic defense deal. Trump said two major U.S. bases. Greenland stays under Danish control.

AFFECTS · GLD · none of the others yet

Learn: the Arctic is becoming a military lane. Gold sometimes ticks when the world arms up. Weak, slow link — not a trade today.

6. Typhoon Dujuan hit eastern Japan. At least six dead, landslides near Tokyo, tens of thousands without power, hundreds of flights canceled.

AFFECTS · none of the six

Learn: a storm can shut airports and still not move the S&P. Worth knowing. Not a VOO buy or sell.

7. Hurricane Polo exploded to Category 5 off Mexico’s Pacific coast. Mostly expected to stay offshore. The Atlantic has not produced a hurricane this season, tied to a strong El Niño.

AFFECTS · none of the six

Learn: two oceans, two seasons. El Niño tends to quiet the Atlantic and juice the eastern Pacific. Do not invent a stock story that is not there.
