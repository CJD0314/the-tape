3. Fed hike hangover - banks dumpedMOVED | JPM | WFC | MS | SCHW | XLF | VOO

Banks live on the gap between short rates and long rates. A fresh hike plus a flat 10-year squeezes that gap. JPM is on our watch list because this channel is not done.

Crude and diesel are not the same product. Cheap crude plus smashed refineries can still mean expensive diesel, which keeps the Fed nervous. That mix can lift oil stocks one day and pressure the whole market the next.
