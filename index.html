This is a notebook, not a trading app. We follow a few construction-related companies and four daily numbers. Most days we do nothing.

WHAT HAPPENED YESTERDAY

Stocks went up. The government’s 10-year borrowing rate eased a little. Oil also came down.

Plain English: money got a bit cheaper, and fuel got a bit cheaper. That is usually friendly for builders and equipment companies. One quiet day is not a reason to buy anything.

WHAT WE DO NEXT Wednesday morning we look at those same four numbers. If nothing loud happened overnight, the $10,000 stays in cash. That is a good outcome.

We ignore everything else on purpose.

HOW THIS WORKS · 30 SECONDS

1. Read the four boxes.

2. Ask: did money get cheaper or more expensive? Did fuel?

3. Only write something down if you can say why in one normal sentence.

4. If you cannot say the sentence, cash stays cash.

The $10,000 is pretend money so we can practice. Never more than $2,000 on one company. Never more than two ideas at once.
