You do not need to know Wall Street words to use this site. Here is the whole idea.

1. WE WATCH A SHORT LIST

Fourteen companies. Machines, stone, home improvement, home builders, oil, a railroad, one fund that owns the whole market, and one fund that moves when interest rates move.

We ignore the other 5,000 stocks on purpose.

2. WE READ FOUR WEATHER REPORTS

Stock market — did the country-wide list of big companies go up or down?

Borrowing cost — the 10-year Treasury. When this number jumps, mortgages and project loans get more expensive. Builders feel it.

Oil — diesel, asphalt, shipping. When this jumps, jobs cost more.

Play money — a pretend $10,000 so we can practice without touching a real account.

3. MOST DAYS WE DO NOTHING

That is the point. A stock can bounce around 1–2% on a normal day for no good reason. We only write something down when we can say why in one normal sentence.

If we cannot say the sentence, cash stays cash.

4. WORDS YOU WILL SEE

PASS — we are not touching it.

LEAN — we have one clean reason. Small pretend bet, or just a note.

BET — rare. Two reasons. Almost never used for a single day.

FADE — stay away. The move already happened or the price is a trap.
