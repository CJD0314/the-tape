THE 14 NAMES

Company name first. Stock letters second. That is all this page is.

MACHINES

Caterpillar (CAT) — the yellow construction machines.

John Deere (DE) — farm and construction equipment.

United Rentals (URI) — they rent the machines instead of selling them.

STONE AND ROADS

Vulcan (VMC) — rock, sand, asphalt.

Martin Marietta (MLM) — same business as Vulcan.

HOUSES

Home Depot (HD) — the store where jobs buy materials.

Lowe’s (LOW) — the other big store.

Lennar (LEN) — they build houses.

D.R. Horton (DHI) — they also build houses.

FUEL, TRAINS, THE WHOLE MARKET

Exxon (XOM) and Chevron (CVX) — oil. Diesel shows up on invoices.

Union Pacific (UNP) — trains that haul freight across the country.

Vanguard S&P 500 (VOO) — one fund that owns a slice of the 500 biggest U.S. companies. This is “the market.”

IEF — a fund that usually goes up when borrowing costs come down.

We are not buying any of these tomorrow morning unless we can say why in one sentence.
